01 — About

More than just making a website.

I care about how a website feels, how quickly someone understands it, and whether it actually helps its owner.

My Development Philosophy

My approach combines clean design, responsive development and practical functionality. I like experimenting with layouts and visual details while keeping the final website usable and lightweight.

I work with WordPress for flexible content-driven websites and use HTML, CSS and JavaScript when a project needs a more custom solution.

What You Can Expect

✦ Responsive thinking — layouts designed for desktop, tablet and mobile.
✦ Visual attention — spacing, typography, colors and hierarchy matter.
✦ Clear communication — requirements first, development second.
✦ Continuous learning — improving both technical and creative skills.
